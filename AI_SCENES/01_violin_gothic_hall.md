---

## Scene Identity

- **Name:** Gothic Concert Hall
- **Instrument:** Violin
- **Atmosphere:** Victorian luxury · Mystery · Warm magic
- **Time of Day:** Late afternoon / golden hour

---

## Environment

```
Victorian concert hall
Dark walnut wood paneling on walls
Tall gothic stained-glass windows (left side of scene)
Window glass: deep blue-green, amber, hints of red
Warm golden sunset light streaming through windows
Wooden floor with subtle golden reflections
High ornate ceiling (partially visible)
Antique bookshelves on far right wall
Violin resting on music stand (foreground left, small)
Sheets of old music notation scattered on a table
Very subtle low fog near floor
Floating dust particles in light beams
```

---

## Camera & Lens

```
35mm equivalent lens
Eye level perspective
No fisheye
No tilt-shift
Natural depth of field
Background slightly soft (not blurred)
```

## Composition

```
LEFT SIDE (58% of canvas): main scene — gothic hall, windows, atmosphere
RIGHT SIDE (42% of canvas): INTENTIONALLY EMPTY — reserved for portrait layer
No objects in the right 42% of composition
Portrait safe zone: right side, vertically centered
Negative space is intentional — do not fill
```

## Light

